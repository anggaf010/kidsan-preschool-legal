Kidsan Preschool is still in development. For help, privacy questions, or account requests, email anggafauzan94@gmail.com.

Before contacting us

Please include the device model, operating-system version, app version, and a short description of what happened. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or a child's private information.

Frequently asked questions

Who owns the account?

The parent or guardian owns and manages it. A child uses a profile beneath that adult account once profiles become available.

How can a parent sign in?

The planned sign-in methods are email and password, Google, and Sign in with Apple on iOS.

Are ads active?

No. The development version does not include the AdMob SDK or any ad unit.

How do I delete an account?

See the account deletion instructions.
