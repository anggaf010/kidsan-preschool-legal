Privacy Policy

Draft for the development version · 22 November 2026

This policy explains how Kidsan Preschool ("Kidsan", "we") handles information. Kidsan is being designed for children to use under a parent or guardian's account. We do not sell personal data. Advertising is not active in the current development version.

Who is responsible

Kidsan is made by MyVoyage in Jakarta, Indonesia. Contact: anggafauzan94@gmail.com.

Information used in the current version

InformationPurpose
Parent or guardian account: email address, internal account ID, and basic identity details provided by Google or Apple when chosen.Create the adult's account, sign them in, and protect access.
Technical error information: app version, device and operating-system details, error messages, and stack traces.Find and fix crashes. Reports are configured to exclude account identity, request details, and interaction breadcrumbs.

Email/password sign-in is processed by Supabase. Google and Apple process their own sign-in credentials; Kidsan does not receive those passwords.

Child profiles and learning progress

Child profiles, mini-game progress, diamonds, and Buddies are planned features and are not active yet. Before collecting that information, we will update this policy with the exact fields, purposes, retention period, parental controls, and deletion process. A child will not be asked to provide an email address or use social sign-in.

Service providers

  • Supabase — authentication and future cloud data storage.
  • Google — optional Sign in with Google for the parent or guardian.
  • Apple — optional Sign in with Apple for the parent or guardian on iOS.
  • Sentry — crash and error reporting with personal-data filtering configured in the app.

Advertising

AdMob app records have been reserved, but the AdMob SDK and ad units are not installed. No ads are shown in the current development version. We will update this policy and complete the required child-audience and consent controls before any advertising is introduced.

Retention and deletion

Account information is kept while the parent or guardian's account exists. A parent or guardian can request deletion using the instructions on the account deletion page. Crash reports may be retained by Sentry according to the project's configured retention period.

Children

A parent or guardian creates and controls the account. Kidsan does not knowingly ask a child for contact details or social-login credentials. If you believe a child submitted personal information without appropriate parental involvement, contact us so we can investigate and delete it.

Security and international processing

Information is transmitted using HTTPS. Service providers may process information in countries outside yours. We use access controls and data minimization, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, export, or delete personal information, or object to or restrict processing. Email us from the address associated with the account.

Changes

We will revise this policy before releasing features that materially change data handling. The date above will be updated when the policy changes.
