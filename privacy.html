Privacy Policy

Updated 24 November 2026

This policy explains how Kidsan Preschool ("Kidsan", "we") handles information. Children use Kidsan under a parent or guardian's account. We do not sell personal data or show advertising.

Who is responsible

Email/password sign-in is processed by Supabase. Google and Apple process their own sign-in credentials; Kidsan does not receive those passwords.

Child profiles and learning progress

A parent can enter a child's nickname and age. The app saves those details, completed rounds, answered questions, and diamonds in its local database first, so it works offline, and uploads them to our backend when the device is online. The uploaded record is linked to the parent or guardian account, not to a device identifier, and is used only to run the app. The app does not yet restore progress from the cloud after a reinstall or on a new device, so reinstalling or clearing the app's data can still erase what the device holds. Signing out leaves the device copy in place. A child is not asked for an email address or social sign-in.

Service providers

  • Supabase — parent authentication, and storage of child profiles and progress linked to the parent account.
  • Google — optional Sign in with Google for the parent or guardian.
  • Apple — optional Sign in with Apple for the parent or guardian on iOS.
  • Sentry — technical crash reporting if enabled in the app.

Advertising

Kidsan does not show ads and does not include the AdMob SDK.

Retention and deletion

Account information is kept while the parent or guardian's account exists. A parent or guardian can request account deletion using the instructions on the account deletion page. Child profiles and progress stored in our backend are kept while the parent or guardian's account exists and are deleted when the account is deleted, including when a parent deletes the account inside the app. Local child records on the device are removed when the account is deleted in the app; otherwise they remain until the app's data is cleared or the app is removed. Crash reports, if collected, follow the service provider's retention setting.

Children

A parent or guardian creates and controls the account. Kidsan does not knowingly ask a child for contact details or social-login credentials. If you believe a child submitted personal information without appropriate parental involvement, contact us so we can investigate and delete it.

Security and international processing

Information is transmitted using HTTPS. Service providers may process information in countries outside yours. We use access controls and data minimization, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, export, or delete personal information, or object to or restrict processing. Email us from the address associated with the account.

Changes

We will revise this policy before releasing features that materially change data handling. The date above will be updated when the policy changes.
