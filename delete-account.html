Delete your account

Applies to Kidsan Preschool by MyVoyage.

Delete inside the app

Open the app, tap the settings icon, pass the parent check, then choose “Delete account” at the bottom of the Profile screen. Type DELETE and confirm. This deletes the account and the child profiles and progress stored for it, and removes the copy on that device.

Or request deletion by email

Email anggafauzan94@gmail.com from the email address used for the parent or guardian account, with the subject “Delete my Kidsan Preschool account.” If Apple Hide My Email was used, send the request from or identify the associated relay address.

We may ask you to confirm ownership of the account. We will process a verified request within 30 days and confirm when it is complete.

What will be deleted

The parent or guardian account and its authentication record, and the child nicknames, ages, learning progress, and diamond balances stored in our backend for that account. Deleting in the app also erases the local copy on that device. Account deletion cannot remotely erase a local copy on another device or after the app was removed; remove the app or clear its data there.

What is not affected

Deleting Kidsan data does not delete the Google or Apple account used to sign in. Deletion cannot be undone.
